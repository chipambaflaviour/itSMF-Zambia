itSMF Zambia is the premier professional body dedicated to the advancement of IT Service Management (ITSM) across the nation.

Our Story

A Legacy of Service Management.

Established to bridge the gap between global ITIL standards and the unique challenges of the Zambian digital landscape, itSMF Zambia began as a collective of visionary IT leaders. Our journey started in response to the growing need for a unified voice that promotes best practices, continuous improvement, and professional integrity.

Today, we stand as the definitive hub for ITSM excellence, fostering a community where professionals can connect, share knowledge, and shape the future of IT services in the region.
